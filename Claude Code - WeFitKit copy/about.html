Who We Are

Experts. Partners.

WeFitKit is a specialist fitness installation and project delivery company focused on creating exceptional fitness environments across the UK.

We combine deep industry experience with a hands-on approach and a genuine pride in the work we deliver. Every project — from a single PT studio to a large-scale commercial facility — receives the same level of commitment and care.

We don't just install equipment. We partner with operators, developers and designers to deliver projects that are on time, on budget, and built to last.
